Why this started
12.17

My grandfather passed away on December 17, 2021. I started Jhatta Foundation so that his memory would turn into something useful for other people.

Each year on that date we hold events in his honor. They aren’t strictly religious. They are a day to gather, remember him, and do the work he would have wanted done.

Raahat JhattaFounder, Jhatta Foundation
